About This Exam

RMS Entrance Exam Kya Hai?

Rashtriya Military Schools (5 schools across India) Defence Ministry ke under aane wale residential institutions hain jo students ko NDA aur defence services ke liye taiyar karte hain. Admission Class 6th aur Class 9th mein hoti hai.

LBK Cadets Pathshala mein Class 3rd se 8th tak ke students ke liye structured academic coaching ke saath physical training bhi di jaati hai — kyunki RMS mein sirf marks nahi, fitness bhi selection criteria hai.

FAQs

RMS Ke Baare Mein Aapke Sawaal

RMS mein kitne schools hain aur admission kaise hota hai?
India mein 5 Rashtriya Military Schools hain. Admission written exam, physical fitness test, medical examination aur interview ke through hota hai.
RMS ke liye kaunsi classes eligible hain?
Class 6th aur Class 9th mein entry hoti hai — iske liye students ko respectively Class 5th ya Class 8th mein padhna zaroori hai.
Physical fitness kitni important hai RMS ke liye?
Bahut important hai — written exam ke saath physical fitness test aur medical bhi selection ka hissa hai. LBK mein daily fitness drills karwaye jaate hain.
