About KV Admission

Kendriya Vidyalaya Admission Process Kya Hai?

Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) mein admission ek priority-based system se hota hai — defence/government employee ke bacchon ko preference milti hai, general category ke liye computerized lottery system follow hota hai. Class 2 aur upar ke liye kabhi-kabhi entrance test bhi hota hai.

LBK Cadets Pathshala mein hum sirf documentation aur process guidance hi nahi, balki students ko strong academic foundation bhi dete hain — taaki KV mein admission ke baad bhi wo top perform karein.

FAQs

KV Admission Ke Baare Mein Aapke Sawaal

KV admission mein priority kisko milti hai?
Defence aur government employee ke bacchon ko sabse pehle priority milti hai, baaki seats general category ke liye computerized lottery se bharti hain.
LBK kya-kya guidance deta hai KV ke liye?
Documentation preparation, online registration guidance, timeline tracking aur saath mein CBSE syllabus ke hisab se academic foundation classes.
Kya har class mein KV admission ho sakta hai?
Zyada tar admission entry-level classes mein hoti hai, lekin agar seats khali hain to upar ki classes mein bhi admission possible hai — subject to availability.
