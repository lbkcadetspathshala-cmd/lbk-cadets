About Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya

JNV Kya Hai?

Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas (JNVs) Bharat Sarkar dwara sthapit fully residential, co-educational schools hain jo talented bacchon ko — khaaskar rural areas se — free, quality education dete hain. Ye Ministry of Education ke under, Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) dwara manage kiye jaate hain. India mein 660+ JNVs, 28 states aur 8 union territories mein phaile hue hain.

Admission Class VI ke liye JNVST se aur Class IX ke liye Lateral Entry Selection Test (LEST) se hota hai (subject to vacancy). Board: CBSE. Medium: Class VI-VIII mother tongue/regional language, Class IX se Maths & Science English mein aur Social Science Hindi mein.

🎯

Quality free education, rural talent ko excellence ka mauka, national integration, discipline aur leadership.

FAQs

JNVST & LEST Ke Baare Mein Aapke Sawaal

Class VI JNVST ke liye age eligibility kya hai?
Candidate ka DOB 1 May 2015 se 31 July 2017 ke beech hona chahiye (2027-28 cycle ke liye), aur wo currently Class V mein same district ke recognized school mein padh raha ho.
Class IX LEST ke liye age eligibility kya hai?
Candidate ka DOB 1 May 2012 se 31 July 2014 ke beech hona chahiye, aur wo currently Class VIII mein padh raha ho. Admission vacancy ke subject hai.
JNVST aur LEST ka exam pattern kya hai?
Class VI (JNVST): 100 marks — Mental Ability (50), Arithmetic (25), Language (25), 2 ghante. Class IX (LEST): 100 marks — English (15), Hindi (15), Maths (35), Science (35), 2.5 ghante. Dono mein negative marking nahi hai.
Kya JNV mein education free hai?
Haan, tuition, boarding, lodging, textbooks, uniform, library aur medical facilities free hain. Sirf Class IX-XII ke general category students ko nominal Vidyalaya Vikas Nidhi (VVN) dena hota hai.
Reservation kaise apply hoti hai?
Minimum 75% seats rural candidates ke liye, 27% OBC (non-creamy layer), SC/ST district population ke hisab se, minimum 1/3 seats girls ke liye, aur Divyang candidates ke liye bhi reservation hai.
LBK mein JNVST/LEST batch kab shuru hoti hai?
Batches saal bhar chalti hain — Class 3rd se 5th tak Class VI JNVST ke liye, aur Class 8th LEST appearing students ke liye. Free counselling ke time exact schedule bata diya jaata hai.
